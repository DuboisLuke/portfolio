# Luke Dubois — Portfolio

Personal portfolio site: GIS maps and planning research. Three pages
(home, GIS Mapping gallery with 23 maps, Reports & Research with 16
reports), styled with a topography-inspired design — contour lines,
deep forest green, warm stone.

## Contents

- `index.html` — home: portrait photo, intro, Jane Jacobs
  quote, links to Reports and Gallery.
- `gallery.html` — all 23 maps, from `assets/gallery/` (15 MB total),
  with a click-to-enlarge lightbox.
- `reports.html` — all 16 reports with links. The SLC bus routes piece
  is an ArcGIS StoryMap link, not a file.
- `style.css` — design, including the gallery lightbox styles.
